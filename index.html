Account prioritization · part of GTM Orbit

Account Focus

Five accounts a day, why now, and the next move.

AI saves sellers almost five hours a week, yet 72% of sales teams don't put that time into high-value selling. A longer ranked list isn't the fix. Five picks a seller can check is.

  • Separates worth winning, moving now and able to reach the buyer, so a hot account with the wrong people says multithread instead of disappearing
  • Reads calls instead of counting tracker hits: our own rep's questions count for nothing
  • Every sentence cites its evidence; a validator removes the rest, and every decision is audited

Sales knowledge · AE succession

SalesLore

Your best sellers' knowledge. Never lost.

The top 14% of sellers generate 80% of revenue, and a new AE takes 6.2 months to ramp. When a top AE leaves, the CRM keeps the record but loses who really decides, what was promised and how they worked.

  • Captures a departing AE's account knowledge and approved working patterns as a context graph in Postgres, with evidence on every edge
  • Agents draft a brief per inherited account; a validator rejects any uncited claim, and only the manager who owns the handoff can approve
  • Stops on do-not-contact, labels stale and unknown facts, filters personal details, and never speaks to customers as the departed rep

Investment intelligence · VC & PE

Conviction AI

From market signal to evidence-backed investment decision.

A deck that claims 124% NRR produces a confident memo that says 124%. The expensive mistakes come from numbers nobody checked.

  • Recomputes the deck's 124% NRR from customer rows (113%) and holds both until a reviewer resolves them
  • NRR, MOIC, IRR and LBO returns come from tested engines, never from the model
  • Anything not public shows as Missing, never guessed or scored by default

Agent marketplace · B2B platform

Meridian

Every agent, governed and paid for in one place.

Every platform is making AI agents easier to buy, yet only 19.7% of tech leaders say all their agents are fully governed before go-live. Meridian makes agents safe to switch on and proves they get used.

  • IT approves once: groups, tools, PII masking and a spend cap apply in chat, the data cloud and Slack
  • A validator rejects uncited claims; stale data and unknowns are flagged, never hidden
  • Writes wait for the person who asked, and the north star is purchase-to-weekly-active, not bookings

Field service · Guided resolution

Fieldwise

Your next move, made clear.

A failed first service visit adds two more visits and 14 days on average. When sibling models share a fault code, a confident answer for the wrong model is how it happens.

  • Gives zero repair steps until a person confirms the model and revision, and never guesses from a serial number
  • Every step cites a manual page and runs from a reviewer-published, version-pinned procedure the technician resumes on a phone
  • Chat can explain a step but never choose a branch; withdrawing a procedure pauses live runs, and every action is audited

Consumer · Payments

Readaroo

Never wait for the next book, and never read alone.

When the book your kid wants is on hold, the reading streak stalls. Readaroo finds similar books you can borrow from your libraries tonight.

  • One holds list across every library card in the family
  • Parents set age bands and approve exceptions; a weekly family reading digest
  • Libraries fund instant borrows, metered through Stripe with a hard cap that's never exceeded
