Conviction AI

From market signal to evidence-backed investment decision.

Agent marketplace · B2B platform

Meridian

Every agent, governed and paid for in one place.

Every platform is making AI agents easier to buy, yet only 19.7% of tech leaders say all their agents are fully governed before go-live. Meridian makes agents safe to switch on and proves they get used.

  • IT approves once: groups, tools, PII masking and a spend cap apply in chat, the data cloud and Slack
  • A validator rejects uncited claims; stale data and unknowns are flagged, never hidden
  • Writes wait for the person who asked, and the north star is purchase-to-weekly-active, not bookings

Field service · Guided resolution

Fieldwise

Your next move, made clear.

A failed first service visit adds two more visits and 14 days on average. When sibling models share a fault code, a confident answer for the wrong model is how it happens.

  • Gives zero repair steps until a person confirms the model and revision, and never guesses from a serial number
  • Every step cites a manual page and runs from a reviewer-published, version-pinned procedure the technician resumes on a phone
  • Chat can explain a step but never choose a branch; withdrawing a procedure pauses live runs, and every action is audited
