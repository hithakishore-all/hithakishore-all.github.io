SalesLore

Your best sellers' knowledge. Never lost.

SalesLore is institutional memory for sales teams. When a top AE leaves, it keeps what they knew about each account and how they worked, as a context graph with evidence on every edge. It turns that into a cited brief for the next seller, which a manager approves. Successors can then ask questions in Slack. Nothing is ever sent to a customer.

What's real in this prototype

A personal prototype: my own idea, designed and built independently to explore it. Here is what it actually does, and what it simulates.

  • BuiltA Node app on Postgres with a context graph, a governed view, the Discover → Research → Compose → Validate agents, owner-only approval that can safely run twice, a stop rule and a privacy filter
  • BuiltA Slack Companion and a web console, 16 automated tests, and a CI check that fails if the seed data drifts
  • ScriptedWording is scripted by default. MODEL=anthropic lets a live model phrase the summary, but it never adds claims. The pattern steps are seeded, not learned
  • SimulatedCall and email signals are fictional rows. There are no real Gong or email connectors yet
  • SyntheticKestrel Cloud and every account, person, quote and number

01Problem and opportunity

Sales knowledge is concentrated in a few people, ramping a replacement takes half a year, and much of what the best sellers know never reaches a system.

  • 14% → 80%the top 14% of sellers generate 80% of revenue (655K opportunities analyzed)Public source Ebsta × Pavilion, 2025 GTM Benchmarks
  • 6.2 monthsaverage time to ramp a new AE, the longest in the study's historyPublic source The Bridge Group, 2026 AE report
  • 42%of institutional knowledge is unique to the individualPublic source Panopto, 2018

The opportunity

Capture what a departing seller knew, with their consent, and turn it into something a successor can act on and a manager can approve.

02Target users

  • Sales manager (transfer owner)Owns the handover and is the only person who can approve a brief.
  • Successor AEInherits the accounts, reads the cited briefs and asks the Companion questions in Slack.
  • Departing AEOpts in, then approves, edits or rejects their own working patterns.

03Product concept and thesis

Keep the knowledge, not the person: evidence for every claim, consent before capture, and no impersonation.

  • Account memory. Who really decides, open promises, risks and next steps, each with a receipt you can open.
  • Seller DNA. The departing seller's approved working patterns, such as clearing security review before pricing.
  • Labeled uncertainty. Unknown, stale and conflicting facts are shown as such, never guessed.

04User journey and workflow

  1. Open the Overview: Maya Okafor's book is moving to Leo Brandt, with each account's readiness.
  2. Read Seller DNA: Maya's approved and rejected pattern steps.
  3. Open an account in Account Memory and click a receipt such as EV-CALL-0412 to see its source.
  4. Go to Transfers → Brightwater Health → Run agents. The validator rejects “Champion has approved budget”, Compose retries, and Dana approves.
  5. Switch to Leo and try to approve: only the transfer owner can.
  6. Ask the Companion “who decides at Halcyon?”, then read the Evidence Ledger.

05Architecture and integrations

SalesforceCalls (simulated)Email (simulated)→Postgres context graph→DiscoverResearchComposeValidate→Manager approves→Slack Companion

Raw records become graph nodes and edges in Postgres, exposed through one governed view. Personal, non-business evidence never appears in that view.

Built with: Node.js · PostgreSQL · Context graph · Slack bot · Salesforce. Full architecture notes are in the repository.

06Important decisions and trade-offs

  • Consent before captureCapture runs only for sellers who opt in and approve their own patterns.Less coverage than silently mining everything, but it's ethical and trusted.
  • Never speak as the departed sellerSalesLore has no send action and never impersonates anyone.It would demo better if it did. It doesn't.
  • Show conflicts side by sideAn email says Q1 budget and a later call says Q2, so both are shown.The successor has to judge, but nothing is quietly overwritten.
  • Postgres over a warehouseIt's free, runs on a laptop, and anyone can reproduce the live version.Porting to Snowflake takes small SQL changes.

08Business value

Hypothesis This is the value expected if a pilot confirms the north star. None of it is a measured outcome.

  • Revenue continuity. The accounts at risk when a top seller leaves are the ones that matter most.
  • Faster ramp. Successors start from cited context instead of rebuilding it.
  • Knowledge as an asset. Working patterns become reusable, with the seller's consent.
