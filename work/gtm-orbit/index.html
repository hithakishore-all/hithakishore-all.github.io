Agentic go-to-market

GTM Orbit

Every account has a next move. Orbit helps you find it.

GTM Orbit turns a ranked account into an outreach brief a seller can send. Salesforce, call and product signals come together in Snowflake. Four bounded agents (Discover, Research, Compose, Validate) build the brief from one governed view, every claim cites its evidence, and the account owner approves it in a web console or in Slack. Nothing is sent automatically.

What's real in this prototype

An independent prototype: conceived and built independently to explore an idea. Here is what it actually does, and what it simulates.

  • BuiltThe four-stage agent flow as a stored procedure inside Snowflake, with a state machine, evidence checks, up to two validator retries, owner-only approval and an audit trail of every step
  • BuiltTwo front ends on one engine: a web console and a Slack bot (/orbit), plus an end-to-end smoke test
  • ScriptedThe agents' wording is pre-written by default, because Snowflake trial accounts block AI functions. One setting switches it to a Snowflake Cortex model
  • SimulatedGong call transcripts and tracker hits
  • SyntheticTen fictional accounts, people and calls. The browser demo replays a snapshot of real runs
  • Not builtSending email. Approval records a decision and never sends anything

01Problem and opportunity

A priority score tells a seller which account. It doesn't say why now, who to contact or what to say. That context is scattered across systems, and drafts that make up facts cost more trust than they save in time.

  • ~40%of a seller's time goes to selling, and 51% of sales leaders using AI say disconnected systems slow it downPublic source Salesforce, State of Sales 2026
  • 69%of B2B buyers check AI-generated insights with a sales repPublic source Gartner, May 2026
  • <40%of sellers are expected to say AI agents improved their productivity, even as agents may outnumber sellers 10 to 1 by 2028 (prediction)Public source Gartner, Nov 2025

The opportunity

The opportunity isn't another generator. It's a workflow where every sentence can be traced to its evidence and the seller stays in control.

02Target users

  • Account executiveWorks a ranked queue, prepares outreach and owns the approval for their accounts.
  • Sales and RevOps leadersOwn the scoring model and need freshness, versioning and an audit trail they can inspect.
  • Security and data ownersNeed agents to read one governed view under a least-privilege service user.

03Product concept and thesis

Not more AI, but context a seller can trust, delivered where they already work, with the seller in control.

  • No uncited claims. Every sentence in a draft points to an evidence ID.
  • Unknown is not low. A stale or missing score shows as stale or not scored, never quietly ranked low.
  • Read, don't recompute. Agents read the scoring model's score, version and freshness, and never re-score accounts.
  • Human approval. Only the account owner can approve, and approving sends nothing.

04User journey and workflow

  1. Open the queue: today's accounts ranked by the scoring model, each marked fresh, stale or not scored.
  2. Pick Northstar Field Services and review its Signals, Buying group and Score tabs. Every piece of evidence shows its source and date.
  3. Click Prepare outreach brief. The orbit diagram lights up as Discover, Research, Compose and Validate run.
  4. Validate rejects “expanding into three new regions”, a claim from an unsourced note, and the rewrite comes back fully cited. A contact who opted out is skipped.
  5. Edit the draft, approve it as the account owner, then read the audit trail of every agent step and decision.
  6. Try Verdant Grid Energy: every contact opted out, so Orbit stops and writes no draft.

Try it yourself in the live demo.

05Architecture and integrations

SalesforceGong (simulated)Product signals→Snowflake governed view→DiscoverResearchComposeValidate→Seller approves

The flow is a Python stored procedure in Snowflake that writes its state and every step to audit tables. A Node.js app (an Express web console and a Slack bot on Bolt in Socket Mode) starts runs, polls their progress and records edits, approvals and rejections. Both front ends share one engine.

Built with: Salesforce · Snowflake · Slack bot · Node.js. Full architecture notes are in the repository.

Stage-by-stage inputs, outputs and guardrails

ValidateDraft plus evidencePass, or flagged claims sent backAt most 2 retries, then it escalates to the seller
ApproveBriefEdit, approve or rejectOwner only. Approval never sends

06Important decisions and trade-offs

  • Make the validator a hard gateA draft that can't cite its evidence is rewritten at most twice, then escalated to the seller.Slower than shipping the first draft, but one invented fact costs more trust than a minute saves.
  • Agents read one governed viewThe app logs in as a key-pair service user that can read views and call procedures, and nothing else.Less flexibility for the agents in exchange for least privilege and auditability.
  • Unknown is not lowStale and unscored accounts are labeled, not sorted to the bottom.The queue looks less tidy, but it's honest about data quality.
  • Scripted wording by defaultEverything except the wording is real, so the demo is reproducible and runs on a free trial.The language is less natural until a live model is switched on.

07Evaluation and success metrics

North star Proposed

Briefs approved by the account owner, and how often the validator has to step in.

Verified today Measured

  • An end-to-end smoke test runs the real server and covers the queue, buying group, a full brief where Validate rejects and then passes, edit, approve, no double approval, the no-contact stop and bad-input errors
Proposed pilot metrics
  • Proposed Approval rate of briefs by account owners
  • Proposed Validator catch rate and retries per brief
  • Proposed Share of accounts stopped for having no permitted contact (a guardrail that should hold at 100%)

The audit trail records every agent step with timings, which is the raw material for these metrics. No real-world results are claimed.

08Business value

Hypothesis This is the value expected if a pilot confirms the north star. None of it is a measured outcome.

  • Seller time. Sellers spend less time stitching together context from several systems before reaching out.
  • Trust and adoption. Cited, validated briefs address the main reason sellers ignore AI output.
  • Governance. Every agent step and human decision is auditable, which matters to RevOps, security and compliance.

09Future opportunities

  • Expose Orbit's actions as MCP tools so a seller can ask Claude in plain English, with Validate and approval still enforced
  • A Salesforce panel on the account page; Hex dashboards for validator catch rate and approval rate; a Streamlit in Snowflake version
  • Switch the agents to a live Cortex model with one setting
  • For production: a real call connector, the scoring model owner's feed, SSO and per-user identity in Slack, hosted authentication, and a pilot in shadow mode first
