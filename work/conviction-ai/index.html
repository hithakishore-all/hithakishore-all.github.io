Conviction AI

From market signal to evidence-backed investment decision.

Conviction AI is an investment-intelligence workspace for venture capital and private equity teams. It follows one deal from “which market is inflecting?” to “should we take this to IC?” and shows where every number came from. When a deck and a company's own data disagree, it keeps both values, recomputes the metric from source rows, drafts the question for the CFO and holds the deal back from IC until a person resolves it.

What's real in this prototype

What this prototype actually does, and what it simulates.

  • BuiltMatching deterministic engines in Python and JavaScript, tested to give the same numbers, for NRR, GRR, runway, TAM, MOIC, IRR, the inflection index and LBO returns
  • BuiltA FastAPI service on a Postgres schema shaped like Salesforce objects, and a rule-based IC recommendation
  • BuiltA Claude research agent (needs an API key). A validator checks that every fact has a source the agent actually found, and private metrics stay Missing
  • ScriptedThe Ask Conviction box and the bear case are rule-based and cite records, so the demo is reproducible offline
  • Public sourceClearOps, ParentSquare and the add-on candidates are real companies researched from public sources, with every fact linked
  • AssumptionAdd-on scores (1 to 5) are analyst judgments with stated reasoning
  • SyntheticHelioOps, Crestline, the funds, rounds and market signals

01Problem and opportunity

Capital is concentrating in AI faster than investment teams can check the stories that attract it. Most AI tools for investors make research faster. Speed isn't the problem.

  • 57%of investors spend 21 or more hours a week on deal researchPublic source Affinity, 275 investors

The opportunity

Build for the opposite job: recompute what can be recomputed, flag what's missing, and keep conflicts visible until a person resolves them.

02Target users

  • Associate or principalRuns diligence: data room, cohort math, evidence chain.
  • Partner and ICNeeds a recommendation backed by evidence coverage, not just a score.
  • PE deal teamUnderwrites a buyout: EBITDA adjustments, leverage, LBO returns.
  • Growth-equity sponsorScreens add-ons and assesses exit readiness after the deal.

03Product concept and thesis

Coverage is separate from score. A company can score well and still be blocked when only 3 of 10 critical claims are backed by evidence.

  • The model never does the math. Language explains; tested engines calculate.
  • Conflicts are kept, never overwritten. Both values stay, marked Disputed, until a reviewer resolves them.
  • Unknown is shown as unknown. A missing metric never counts as a pass or gets a default score.
  • Rule-based recommendation. More diligence below 65% coverage, Advance with conditions below 85% or while blockers remain, and Advance to IC only when both clear.

04User journey and workflow

  1. Opportunity Radar: six AI sub-segments scored on an inflection index. Leave out the top 5 mega-rounds and the #1 segment changes.
  2. Fund Thesis: edit the mandate's rules and every deal re-scores.
  3. Company 360 → HelioOps: load the data room. NRR is recomputed at 113.0% against the deck's 124%, and both values are held.
  4. Decision & IC Memo: make the bear case, then prepare a sourced IC memo.
  5. PE mode: in LBO-lite, pricing on seller-adjusted EBITDA cuts IRR from 15.9% to 9.9% and pushes leverage past the 4.0x cap.
  6. ClearOps (researched): built from five public sources. Six metrics are Missing and three facts are Disputed, so coverage is 30% and the recommendation is More diligence.

05Architecture and integrations

Data room rowsPublic sources→Postgres (Salesforce-shaped)→Deterministic engine→Coverage + blockers→Human decision

The UI is a single self-contained HTML file. It runs offline on bundled data, or live against a FastAPI service with Postgres, and one module is the only code that talks to the server. Researched companies are imported as validated JSON packs.

Built with: PostgreSQL · FastAPI · React · Claude research agent. Full architecture notes are in the repository.

06Important decisions and trade-offs

  • Keep the model out of the mathEvery figure comes from a tested engine, and two engines in two languages are tested to agree.Twice the code to maintain, but the numbers are reproducible.
  • Hold both values when sources disagreeNothing is quietly overwritten. The reviewer's resolution is recorded alongside the original.More friction in the workflow, by design.
  • Mirror Salesforce objectsFirms already on Salesforce could sync it field for field.The schema is more verbose than a purpose-built one.
  • Use AI where it helps: researchThe agent finds public facts with sources. Private financials stay Missing and drive a data-room request.Researched packs need human review before anyone relies on them.

08Business value

Hypothesis This is the value expected if a pilot confirms the north star. None of it is a measured outcome.

  • Fewer expensive mistakes. Contradicted metrics are caught before IC, not after close.
  • Faster, focused diligence. Gaps turn directly into a data-room request.
  • Post-deal value. The same evidence rules apply to add-on screening and exit readiness.
