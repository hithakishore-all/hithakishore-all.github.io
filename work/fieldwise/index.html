Fieldwise

Your next move, made clear.

Fieldwise carries one equipment problem from the support call to a verified fix on a technician's phone. It gives no repair steps until a person confirms the exact model and revision. After that, every step is cited to a manual page, pinned to a reviewer-approved procedure version and recorded in an audit trail.

What's real in this prototype

An independent prototype: conceived and built independently to explore an idea. Here is what it actually does, and what it simulates.

  • BuiltA FastAPI server on Postgres (with a SQLite fallback): an assistant, a pathway executor, imports, review and publish with separation of duties, and an outbox that delivers each update exactly once
  • BuiltA phone run view that resumes the same pinned run after a refresh or handoff
  • ScriptedThe assistant uses scripted retrieval over stored passages plus response templates. There's no language model in v0.1
  • SimulatedThe “Northline Service Desk” host CRM is a mock page that embeds the workspace
  • SyntheticEvery elevator model, manual, procedure, company and person
  • ProposedAll pilot metrics in the PRD

01Problem and opportunity

When sibling models share a fault code, a confident answer for the wrong model sends a technician down the wrong path, and the customer waits for a second and third visit.

  • +2 visits, +14 daysthe average cost of a failed first visitPublic source Aquant 2025 Field Service Benchmark
  • 86% vs 53%first-time fix rate for top performers against the bottom groupPublic source Aquant 2025 benchmark
  • 30%of solutions appear only in data provided by expertsPublic source Aquant Service Co-Pilot release

The opportunity

Don't add another AI answer. Carry the approved next step, pinned to the right model, from the call to the fix.

02Target users

  • Service desk agent (Ana)Takes the call, confirms the model and revision, starts and assigns a run.
  • Field technician (Teo)Follows the pinned procedure step by step on a phone and records the verified outcome.
  • Knowledge author (Ari)Imports manuals and drafts procedures from templates.
  • Reviewer (Ren)Publishes or withdraws procedure versions, and can't publish their own drafts.

03Product concept and thesis

Chat explains; people choose. The assistant can explain the current step but can never pick a branch.

  • Refuse to guess. No model-specific steps until a person confirms the model and revision, and never inferred from a serial number.
  • Only approved steps. A past fix can support a hypothesis, but only a published, applicable procedure authorizes a step.
  • Pin and resume. A run is pinned to one procedure version. Withdrawing that version pauses live runs.

04User journey and workflow

  1. Ana opens case C104: “doors keep reopening, D17”, with the model and revision unknown.
  2. She clicks Ask. D17 appears for two models, so Fieldwise asks her to confirm, and gives zero steps.
  3. She confirms Model B, rev 2. Now she gets manual MB2 · rev 2 · p14, one verified recovery in two matching cases, and procedure PB2 v1.
  4. She assigns the run to Teo. On his phone, he steps through it, reloads, and the same run, version and step come back.
  5. Teo saves a verified outcome. Ana approves it, a simulated host outage fails the first send, and the retry delivers exactly one summary.
  6. Ren opens Review to see every step, piece of evidence and audit event in order. Withdrawing v1 mid-run pauses Teo's run.

05Architecture and integrations

Case (host CRM, mocked)→Confirm model + revision→Assistant cites passages→Pinned procedure run→Verified outcome→Outbox (exactly once)

A FastAPI server handles sessions and access, the assistant, the pathway executor, imports and the outbox, on Postgres (or SQLite as a fallback). A mock host page embeds the workspace and receives the summary.

Built with: Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Product hierarchy · Procedure templates. Full architecture notes are in the repository.

06Important decisions and trade-offs

  • Refuse to guessZero model-specific steps until a person confirms the exact model and revision.One more question for the agent, instead of a wrong visit.
  • Separate explaining from decidingThe assistant explains steps but can't choose a branch.Less “magical”, and much safer.
  • Pin runs to a versionA procedure update mid-run doesn't change the steps under a technician's feet. A withdrawal pauses the run.Version management for reviewers.
  • Exactly-once deliveryRetry keys, version checks and an outbox mean a double tap or a retry counts once.More infrastructure than a simple API call.

08Business value

Hypothesis This is the value expected if a pilot confirms the north star. None of it is a measured outcome.

  • Fewer repeat visits. Each avoided failed visit saves two more visits and about two weeks.
  • Expert knowledge, governed. Procedures are reviewed, versioned and cited, not improvised.
  • Clean records. One verified summary reaches the host system per closure.
